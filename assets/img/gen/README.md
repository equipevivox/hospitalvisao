## Shared prompt direction

Premium editorial high-end eyewear/lifestyle brand campaign film-like photography for ophthalmology hospital in Cuiabá Brazil. Warm natural light, cream #F4EFE6, amber/gold #E49F47, copper #B96B45 and deep charcoal #2B2522 palette, shallow depth of field and soft grain, realistic textures. NO text, NO logos, NO watermarks, no visible brand names. 

The subject prompts above were combined with this shared art direction and the requested aspect ratios. Medical prompts specified calm, realistic scenes without needles, blood, or visible procedures. The final operating room prompt excluded screens and visible patient detail. The glasses prompt requested a transparent isolated product shot; its alpha channel was preserved, with transparent canvas added to reach 1600 × 1000. The sunset prompt requested a wide 21:9 Cerrado landscape with blurred foliage and space for a white text overlay; no text was generated.

## Export

Final images were resized with high-quality bicubic interpolation; photographs received minimal centered cropping to match the target ratios and JPEG quality 90. Portraits and the wide background were modestly upscaled from the tool output to the requested approximate delivery sizes. The transparent product image was scaled to fit without cropping. No HTML or CSS files were changed.
